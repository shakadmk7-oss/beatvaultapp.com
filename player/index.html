BeatVault Music Player

An offline music player for Android. Your music, your folders, no streaming and no ads. BeatVault plays the MP3s and other tracks already on your phone and gives you the controls a DJ would want.

Beta on Google Play
  • Your library, organisedBrowse by songs, artists, albums, genres, years and playlists, and sort up to three levels deep.
  • Edit tags and coversTitle, artist, album, genre, BPM, key, year and artwork, one song or many at once.
  • Identify songs onlineFill in missing details and cover art in one tap.
  • Find duplicatesSpot repeated songs, even renamed files, and compare them before deleting.
  • BPM and key toolsTap out a tempo or analyse a song's key, right in the editor.
  • Sync & RunLock every song to one target BPM for workouts, runs and sets.
  • Smooth transitionsFades and crossfades between songs.
  • True shuffleEvery song plays before any repeats.
  • Music videos tooPlay MP4 music videos and M4A files as songs.
  • PlaylistsBuild your own and export them as M3U8 for other apps.
  • Make it yoursThemes, custom colours, or your own photo as the background.
  • Listening statsYour top songs, artists and albums, and how long you've listened.
  • Controls everywhereLock screen, Bluetooth headphones and Android Auto in the car.
  • Finds your music for youLet it look through your phone, or pick the folders and songs yourself.
