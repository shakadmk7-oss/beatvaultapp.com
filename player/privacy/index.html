Privacy Policy

BeatVault Music Player (Android) · Last updated 9 October 2026

BeatVault Music Player ("the app") is made by Shaka D. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Everything stays on your device

The app has no server, no account and no sign-in. Your music library, playlists, liked songs, listening stats, theme and profile picture are stored only on your phone and are never uploaded.

Your music files

With your permission, the app finds the music on your phone (either by looking through your phone's music, or in the folders and songs you choose) so it can play it. It reads each file's tags, such as title, artist, album, genre, BPM, key, year and artwork. When you edit a song's details or cover, the app writes those changes into the file itself. Your music files never leave your phone.

Online song details and covers

Only when you choose Identify Song Online or Search Online, the app sends the search words you see (such as artist, title, album and year) to MusicBrainz (musicbrainz.org) and downloads cover images from the Cover Art Archive (coverartarchive.org, hosted by archive.org). If a cover can't be loaded directly, the app fetches the same image through the wsrv.nl image service. Like any website, these services see your IP address, and wsrv.nl keeps its request logs for 7 days. No account, song file or other personal data is sent.

Free trial and purchase

The app includes a 14-day free trial. Its start date (and whether you've bought the Lifetime Unlock) is stored in a small file on your device. This one file is included in Android's own device backup, so the trial can't be restarted by reinstalling; your library is not backed up. When online, the app makes one request to a public time service (worldtimeapi.org) to check the real date; no personal data is sent. The optional Lifetime Unlock is bought through Google Play, which handles payment under Google's own privacy policy. The app never sees your payment details.

Diagnostic log

The app keeps a short log of player events (such as play, pause and seek) on your device for up to 24 hours, to help fix problems. It never leaves your phone unless you choose Support > Report a Bug, which opens your own email app with the log attached so you can see the email before you send it.

No analytics, ads or tracking

The app contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools. Nothing about how you use it is collected, and nothing is sold or shared.

Permissions

The app asks for access to your music files, permission to keep playing in the background with media controls on the lock screen and in the car, and, optionally, permission to show notifications. You can withdraw these at any time in Android Settings.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android Settings → Apps → BeatVault, removes your library, playlists, stats and settings. Your music files are not touched. If Android's device backup is on, the small trial file may be restored when you reinstall, so the free trial can't be restarted.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
