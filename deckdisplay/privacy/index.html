Privacy Policy

BeatVault DeckDisplay (Windows) · Last updated 18 November 2026

BeatVault DeckDisplay ("the app") is made by BeatVault (Shaka D). This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: it reads your DJ software on your PC, and only sends something off your PC for the licence check, for missing covers (if that setting is on), and for Discord and Twitch if you connect them.

Your DJ software

To know what you're playing, the app reads the play history that Serato DJ, VirtualDJ and Rekordbox keep on your PC: track names, artist, album, BPM, key, deck, length and the music file's location. It reads the cover, BPM and key from your music files. Rekordbox keeps its library locked, so the app unlocks it to read it, the same way Rekordbox does; it never changes it. None of this leaves your PC, except as described below.

Controller and sound

  • Controller (optional): if you teach the app your DJ controller, it listens to the controller's fader and button messages while it runs, only to tell when a deck goes up or down.
  • Music check: to hide the card when the music stops, the app reads how loud your PC's sound outputs are (a level, not the sound). Sound is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

Backdrop while you design

To show your stream behind the designer, you can pick your streaming app's virtual camera, an app window, or (for OBS) the scene on air. The app shows these pictures inside the app only, while you design. They are never recorded, saved or sent anywhere, and never appear on your stream. Windows may ask before the app can use a camera; you can change that in Windows Settings → Privacy & security → Camera.

Local connection for your streaming app

The app runs a small local server on your PC, at an address starting http://127.0.0.1, so OBS, Streamlabs, vMix or a browser on the same PC can show the overlay. It only accepts connections from your own PC. It can't be reached from the internet or your local network, unless you switch on Two-PC setup (Setup tab, version 1.0.5 and later, off by default). Then a second PC on your home network can load the overlay, and nothing else: only devices on a private home network, only the overlay pages (never the app's settings), and only with the key in the address DeckDisplay shows you. Make a new key at any time to stop the old address working. The internet still can't reach it. Add to OBS and the OBS backdrop talk to OBS on your PC through OBS's own remote control (WebSocket), using the password you type in.

Settings and files on your PC

  • Your settings and Looks, the OBS password, the list of tracks you've hidden, your set history (one file per day), covers found online and a short log are stored on your PC only.
  • Text files (off until you turn them on) are written to Documents\BeatVault DeckDisplay or the folder you choose.
  • Report a bug saves a zip on your Desktop with your description, the app version, what the app sees, the log and your settings, with the OBS password and Discord webhook removed. It is only sent if you attach it to an email yourself.

Missing covers (on by default, can be turned off)

When a track's file has no cover, the app looks it up on MusicBrainz and the Cover Art Archive, free public music databases. Only the artist and title are sent, and covers found are kept on your PC so each track is looked up once. These services see your PC's internet address, like any website. Turn this off in Setup → Covers.

Discord and Twitch (only if you connect them)

  • Discord: if you add a Discord webhook, the app posts the tracks or tracklist you choose to that channel. The webhook address is kept on your PC.
  • Twitch: if you connect Twitch, you sign in on Twitch's own website; the app never sees your password. It keeps Twitch's sign-in tokens on your PC, reads your channel's chat to answer commands like !song, and posts answers (and, if you choose, each track) as you. What's posted is public in your chat. Disconnect any time in the Share tab or in your Twitch settings.

What you post through Discord and Twitch is handled under their own privacy policies.

Free trial and licence key

The app includes a 28-day free trial. After that, it needs a licence key, which you buy from us separately.

  • Machine ID: the app shows a short code, your Machine ID, so we can make a licence key that works on your PC. It is made by one-way scrambling (hashing) an ID that Windows gives your PC, so it can't be turned back into that ID and doesn't identify you. The Buy button adds it to the checkout link, so it reaches us with your order, and the licence check below sends it too.
  • Trial date: the date your trial started is stored on your PC only, so the app can count the 28 days.
  • Licence key: your key contains the name you bought under, your Machine ID and the date it was issued. It is saved on your PC and checked there.

Licence check

So that a purchase unlocks the app by itself, and a refunded purchase is removed, the app asks our licence server (license.beatvaultapp.com) what your PC should have: when it starts, every few hours while it runs, when you open the licence box, and often for a while after you click Buy. Each check sends only your Machine ID and your current licence key, and gets back an updated key if there is one. It sends nothing about your music, your sets or your stream. If your PC is offline, the app keeps working as before and checks again later.

The server keeps, for each purchase, your Machine ID, what you bought, your name and email address as given at checkout, the amount and whether it was refunded, so it can issue and withdraw keys. It runs on Cloudflare, which stores it for us.

What we collect

We collect nothing beyond the licence check above: no account sign-up, no advertising and no analytics. The cover lookups, Discord and Twitch go straight from your PC to those services, not to us.

If you buy a licence, you give us your name, your email address and your Machine ID, and Stripe, which handles the checkout, confirms your order to us. We use these only to issue your licence key, re-issue it if you change PC, answer your questions and keep the business records UK tax law requires. We keep sale records for as long as that law requires (currently about 6 years), then delete them. We never sell or share them, except with Stripe for your purchase, Cloudflare to run the licence server, and when the law requires it.

Payments are handled by Stripe, which sells the licence on our behalf (it appears at checkout and on bank statements as "Onelink") and collects any sales tax or VAT. Stripe processes your payment details under its own privacy policy. We never see your full card or bank details.

Microsoft services

  • Microsoft Store: you download and update the app through the Microsoft Store. The app doesn't contact the Store itself, and buying a licence doesn't go through the Store. See the Microsoft Privacy Statement.
  • Microsoft Edge WebView2: the app uses this Windows component to show its studio. WebView2 is provided and updated by Microsoft under the same privacy statement.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes your settings, Looks, set history, covers, log, trial date and licence key. Text files and bug report zips you made stay where they were saved until you delete them. If you reinstall on the same PC, the app gets your licence again by itself.

To see or delete the sale record we hold about you, email us. We'll delete everything except what UK tax law requires us to keep.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.
