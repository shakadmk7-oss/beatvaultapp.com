BeatVault Visualiser

A music and audio visualizer for your Windows PC. Turn whatever's playing into live visuals that react to the beat. Made for DJ sets, live streams in OBS and a big screen at home.

Available on the Microsoft Store
  • Reacts to any sound on your PCPick any playback device: speakers, headphones, HDMI, USB or Bluetooth. DJ software, Spotify, YouTube, games, anything playing. No "Stereo Mix" or extra hardware needed.
  • Add your microphoneOptionally mix in a mic, with its own level control and meter.
  • Beat detectionVisuals pulse with the bass, the beat and the treble, with an optional beat flash.
  • 16 effects to layerCosmic Background, Sparkles, Rain, Matrix, 3D Grid, Neon Tunnel, Wormhole, Embers, VHS Retro and more. Most can run in reverse.
  • Halloween effect pack17 spooky extras: Wraiths, Watchers, Hallway Figure, Blood Moon, Zombie Hands, Raven Storm, Ghosts, Pumpkins and more. All see-through, so they layer over your stream. Try it free for 48 hours once you own the app, then £1.99.
  • Infinite Loops effect pack12 endless tunnel and symmetry effects: Kaleido Tunnel, Mirror Room, Fractal Zoom, Speed Warp, Hex Tunnel, Star Gate, Spiral Tunnel, Neon Corridor and more. Made to loop forever on a projector or behind your stream. Try it free for 48 hours once you own the app, then £2.99.
  • Your coloursFull colour control, a black-and-white mode and saved colour schemes.
  • Show it anywhereLive preview in the app, plus a link you can add to streaming apps like OBS, Streamlabs, XSplit or vMix, or open full screen in a browser on a second screen, TV or projector.
  • Private by designAudio is analysed on your PC and never recorded or sent anywhere.

Photosensitivity warning: the Visualiser shows flashing lights, strobing effects and fast-moving or repeating patterns, which may trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy or other photosensitive conditions. If you or anyone watching could be affected, seek medical advice before use and only continue if it is safe to do so.
