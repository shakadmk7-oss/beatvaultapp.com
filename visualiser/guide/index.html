Visualiser Guide

How to get started and get the most out of BeatVault Visualiser on Windows. Something not working? See Help, or email hello@beatvaultapp.com.

Getting started

Install and first start
  1. Get BeatVault Visualiser from the Microsoft Store and open it. Everything works free for 14 days.
  2. Read the photosensitivity warning and click I understand. It only shows once.
  3. Under Playback device, pick what you hear your music through: speakers, headphones, TV, USB or Bluetooth device, or your DJ controller.
  4. Optional: under Microphone, pick a mic so the visuals react to your voice too. Windows asks your permission first. Use Gain to set how strongly it moves the visuals.
  5. Click Start. Play some music and the preview starts moving.

The top left shows how many trial days are left. Click it any time to buy a licence or get help.

What the buttons along the top do
  • Mute (next to a device): stops that device moving the visuals. Your speakers and mic aren't affected.
  • Cog (next to a device): opens Windows sound or microphone settings.
  • Refresh devices: use it after plugging in a new speaker, headset or mic.
  • Font size: makes the app's text bigger or smaller.
  • ? (Help and support): your Machine ID, these help pages and email support.
  • The BeatVault logo opens this website.

Showing the visuals

Full screen on this PC

Double-click the preview to fill the screen. Double-click again, or press Esc, to come back.

On a second screen, TV or projector
  1. Connect the screen or projector and set Windows to Extend the display (Windows key + P).
  2. Under the look you want, find For a second screen, TV or projector and click Open. It opens in your web browser with a black background.
  3. Drag that window onto the second screen, then double-click it or press F11 for full screen.

Keep the Visualiser open with Start pressed: the browser window gets the music from it.

Add to OBS in one click
  1. Open OBS (version 28 or later). In OBS, go to Tools, WebSocket Server Settings and tick Enable WebSocket server.
  2. In the Visualiser, press Start, then click Add to OBS under the preview.
  3. If OBS has a password, a box appears. Copy the password from OBS (Tools, WebSocket Server Settings, Show Connect Info), paste it in and click Add to OBS again.

The Visualiser adds a BeatVault Visualiser Browser source to the scene that is on air, at your OBS canvas size. A tall OBS canvas gets the Vertical setup, a wide one the Landscape setup.

Landscape and Vertical canvases

The Landscape and Vertical tabs above the preview hold two setups: one for usual streams and screens, and one for TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Twitch vertical. Each has its own size and its own layer positions. Settings marked ⇅ are set separately for each canvas.

In OBS, Streamlabs, XSplit or vMix
  1. Under the look you want, find For OBS and other streaming apps and click Copy.
  2. In your streaming app, add a Browser source and paste the link as its URL. Set the size to match your canvas, for example 1920 by 1080.
  3. The background is see-through, so you can put the visuals over a camera, a game or an image.

Keep the Visualiser open with Start pressed while you stream. If the source goes blank, see Help.

Effects and colours

Main visualisers

Pick the look that moves to the music from the Main visualiser list at the top of the left panel. Its own settings are underneath. To run more than one, click + Add another visualiser layer under the settings. Each one you add has its own look, settings and layer, and a Remove button. Add as many as you like.

Layers and placing things on the preview
  • Layers: every visualiser and effect that is on shows in the Layers list. The top one draws over the rest. Click and hold a layer and drag it up or down to change the order, and set each layer's visibility, opacity (in 1% steps, shown on the preview as you drag) and blend mode.
  • On the preview: click a layer on the preview to pick it, then drag it to move it or drag its handles to resize it. It snaps to the edges and centre lines, and Fill screen and Centre put it back quickly. Click anywhere else to let go of it. Positions are kept separately for the Landscape and Vertical canvases.
  • Turning a layer: drag the round handle above the picked layer. It snaps every 45°; hold Shift for 15° steps. Double-click the handle or press Straighten to level it again.
  • Glow past the edges: a resized layer's glow, beams and sparkles can spill past its box. Tick Crop to box on its layer to cut everything off at the box's edges instead, for example for snow falling in from above.
  • Undo and redo: Ctrl+Z undoes your last change and Ctrl+Y (or Ctrl+Shift+Z) redoes it. The arrows at the top of Layers do the same.
Adding and layering effects

The effects list is on the left. Tick an effect to add it; tick several to layer them. Most effects show their own settings when ticked, such as size, speed, direction or colour.

Effects marked with * react to the music; the others move on their own.

Colours, the eyedropper and saved schemes
  • Main palette: the colours for bass, mid, treble and peaks. Click a colour box to change it, and set its opacity in the colour window.
  • Eyedropper: in the colour window, use the eyedropper to copy a colour from anywhere on your screen, such as your logo.
  • Beat flash adds a flash of colour on the beat. Monochrome turns everything black and white.
  • Saved colour schemes: type a name and click Save to keep your colours, then pick it later to switch back. Reset all colours returns to the defaults.
Saving and sharing Looks

Open Looks on the left panel, type a name and click Save. A Look keeps everything: effects, settings, colours and both canvases. Pick it later to switch back. Export saves the chosen Look as a file to keep or share, and Import adds a Look from a file.

Effect packs

Trying and buying an effect pack

Effect packs, like Halloween, Infinite Loops, Winter Festive and Diwali Celebration, add extra effects. Each pack in the effects list shows where it stands:

  • ⏱ Try free or the time left: tick any of its effects to start a free 48-hour trial. You can try packs during your 14-day app trial too.
  • 🛒 Get: buy the pack. Packs add on to the full Visualiser, so if you haven't bought the Visualiser yet, Get explains that and offers it first.
  • A green ✓ after the pack name: the pack is yours.

After you pay, keep the Visualiser open: the pack unlocks by itself within a minute. If a trial runs out while one of its effects is on screen, it carries on until you switch it off, so your show is never cut.

Licence and support

Buying the Visualiser
  1. Click the trial text at the top left (or ?), then Buy a licence. The checkout opens in your browser.
  2. Pay. Your Machine ID is sent with the order automatically.
  3. Leave the Visualiser open. Within a minute it unlocks by itself and the top left shows Licensed to your name. If it was closed, it unlocks the next time you open it.

It's a one-off payment for this PC, with no subscription.

Moving to a new PC or reinstalling Windows

A licence belongs to one PC. Reinstalling the app on the same PC keeps it. For a new PC or a fresh Windows install, email us your old and new Machine IDs (click ? in the app to see it) and we'll move your licence.

Refunds and getting help

Refunds within 14 days, no questions asked: email hello@beatvaultapp.com. For help, click ? in the app, then Help pages or Email support, and include your Machine ID.
