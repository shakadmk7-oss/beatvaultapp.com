Privacy Policy

BeatVault Visualiser (Windows) · Last updated 8 October 2026

BeatVault Visualiser ("the app") is made by BeatVault (Shaka D). This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Audio

To create visuals that react to your music, the app listens to audio on your PC. Depending on the source you choose, this is your microphone or the sound your PC is playing.

  • Audio is analysed in memory, in real time, only to drive the visuals.
  • Audio is never recorded, saved to disk or sent anywhere.
  • Windows asks for your permission before the app can use the microphone. You can turn this off at any time in Windows Settings → Privacy & security → Microphone.

Local connection for OBS and preview

The app runs a small local server on your PC, at an address starting http://127.0.0.1, so the preview and streaming tools like OBS can show the visuals. This server only accepts connections from your own PC. It can't be reached from the internet or your local network.

Settings

Your preferences, such as your chosen audio devices, microphone level, display options and colour schemes, are stored on your PC only.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no account sign-up, no advertising and no analytics. We don't collect, sell or share any personal data.

Microsoft services

  • Microsoft Store: the free trial, purchase and licence are handled by the Microsoft Store. The app asks the Store whether your licence is a trial or full version. We never see your payment details. See the Microsoft Privacy Statement.
  • Microsoft Edge WebView2: the app uses this Windows component to display the visuals. WebView2 is provided and updated by Microsoft under the same privacy statement.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes its settings from your PC.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
